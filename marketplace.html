    Marketplace

    Player-to-player trading. List items from your inventory, browse the order book, and buy at the best available price — one row per item with multiple price tiers.

    P2PPlayer trading
    📈Price sparklines
    🪙Gold currency

    📑 Marketplace Tabs

    The Marketplace has four tabs:

    TabWhat it shows
    ListingsOrder book — all active listings grouped by item, sorted by price
    SellYour inventory — click any item to open the List for Sale modal
    My ListingsYour active listings with a Cancel button for each
    HistoryRecently completed trades (buyer, seller, price, quantity, time)

    📖 Order Book

    The Listings tab shows an order book: one row per item type. Expand a row to see all available price tiers. Each tier shows the price per unit, total available quantity, and a price sparkline showing the price history over time.

    Floor Price

    The lowest price available for an item across all sellers. The floor price badge is shown prominently on each item row — this is what you'll pay if you hit the Buy button without expanding the tiers.

    Price Sparkline

    Each item row includes a mini sparkline chart showing recent price history. Green = price trending up, red = trending down. Hover for exact values.

    Partial Buys

    When a listing has multiple units available, you can buy a partial quantity. Enter how many you want — you only pay for what you buy. The listing remains active for other buyers.

    💰 Listing Items for Sale

    Open the Sell tab, then click any item in your inventory to open the List for Sale modal.

    1
    Go to Marketplace → Sell tab. Your inventory is displayed.
    2
    Click the item you want to sell. A modal opens showing the current floor price.
    3
    Set your price per unit and quantity. You can't list an item that's currently equipped (it subtracts 1 from your sellable quantity).
    4
    Click List for Sale. The item is moved to the marketplace. It appears instantly in the Listings tab.
    5
    Manage active listings in My Listings. Click Cancel to remove a listing and return the item to your inventory.

    🚫 Non-Tradeable Items

    The following consumable items cannot be listed on the marketplace:

    🧪 ATK Potion ❤️ HP Potion 💚 Regen Potion 🛡️ DEF Potion ☠️ Death Insurance

    Consumable potions and Death Insurance are bound to the player who earned them and cannot be transferred. All equipment (head, top, accessory, aura), materials, farming items, and chests are tradeable.

    🪙 Currency

    All trades use gold as the currency. Gold is earned by killing mobs in the Arena, completing dungeons, and from quest chests. Your current gold balance is displayed in the top-right of the Marketplace page.

    SourceHow to earn gold
    Arena Mob KillsEvery mob kill drops gold based on zone tier
    Dungeon ClearsBonus gold on dungeon completion
    Quest ChestsOpen chests from daily/weekly quests for gold rolls
    Marketplace SalesReceive gold when a buyer purchases your listing
