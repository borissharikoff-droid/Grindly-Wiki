    Friends & Social

    Add friends, watch their activity in real time, compare stats, chat, climb the leaderboard, and team up in guilds and raids.

    👥Friends list
    📊Leaderboard
    🏰Guilds & Raids

    📑 Friends Page Tabs

    Tab / SectionWhat it contains
    FriendsFriend list with activity feed, online status, pending requests
    LeaderboardGlobal and friends-only XP ranking by total skill level
    GuildYour guild panel or guild browser — see Guilds
    PartyRaid party panel — invite friends to an active raid

    ➕ Adding Friends

    Search for any player by their username and send a friend request. They'll see a pending request at the top of their Friends tab.

    Send a Request

    Click Add Friend in the Friends tab. Type the player's exact username and hit Send. The request appears in their Pending Requests list.

    Accept / Decline

    Pending requests appear at the top of your Friends tab. Accept to add them as a friend, or decline to dismiss the request.

    Remove a Friend

    Open a friend's profile and select Remove Friend. This removes the connection for both players.

    Friend Profile

    Click any friend to open their profile: level, skills, equipped gear, active session status, and options to chat, compare, or invite to guild/raid.

    📡 Activity Feed

    The main friend list shows a live activity feed — events from your friends appear as they happen. Feed events include:

    ⚔️
    Boss Kill — friend defeated a boss in the arena (shows boss name and zone)
    🏆
    Achievement Unlocked — friend earned a new achievement (shows icon, name, XP reward)
    💎
    Rare Drop — friend received an epic, legendary, or mythic item from a chest
    ⬆️
    Level Up — friend gained a level in any skill
    🌿
    Farm / Craft events — rare harvests and notable crafts

    📊 Compare Stats

    Open a friend's profile and tap Compare to view a side-by-side skill comparison. Each skill bar shows your XP vs theirs, with color coding for who's ahead.

    💬 Chat

    Open a friend's profile and tap Message to start a direct message thread. Unread badge appears on the Friends tab nav icon when you have new messages.

    🏅 Leaderboard

    The Leaderboard tab ranks players by total skill level (sum of all skill levels). Switch between the global leaderboard and a friends-only view.

    ColumnDescription
    RankPosition in the leaderboard (1 = highest total level)
    PlayerAvatar, username, and title
    Total LevelSum of all skill levels across all 13 skills
    Top SkillThe skill with the highest level for that player

    🔗 Related Features

    Guilds

    Create or join a guild from the Friends → Guild tab. Guild members share passive +5% XP and +5% gold buffs and can pool gold in the guild chest.

    Raids & Party

    Invite friends to join your active raid from the Friends → Party panel. Party members can attack the same raid boss and share in the rewards.
