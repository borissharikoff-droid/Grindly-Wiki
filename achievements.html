    Achievements

    Unlock cosmetic rewards — avatars, profile frames, badges, and skill boosts — by hitting milestones across all game systems. Achievements are checked automatically.

    40+Achievements
    🖼️Frames & Avatars
    ⚡XP Rewards

    Reward Types

    Avatar Emoji avatar shown on your profile
    Frame Profile frame border (diamond, crown, ember, blaze, inferno, code, art, forge, garden, etc.)
    Badge Badge displayed on your profile card
    Skill Boost Bonus XP added directly to a skill
    Title Title shown under your username
