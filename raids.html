    Raids

    Multi-day co-op boss hunts. Invite party members, burn tribute items to open the gate, and deal daily damage until the boss falls — or time runs out.

    ⚔️ How Raids Work

    A raid is a persistent boss fight that lasts multiple days. Each day you launch a normal arena fight against the raid encounter — if you win, your damage chips away at the raid boss's massive HP pool. The raid is won when the total HP reaches 0 before the timer expires.

    Daily Flow

    1. Start the Raid — meet all requirements, burn tribute items, raid is created
    2. Invite Party — send invites to friends via the Friends tab; they join the same raid
    3. Daily Attack — each party member fights the encounter once per day; winning deals contribution_per_win damage to the boss
    4. Watch the Phase — boss ATK increases as its HP drops (3 phases)
    5. Claim Victory — when boss HP = 0 the raid is won; click Claim to collect chest + exclusive loot chance
    6. Raid Fails — if the timer expires before HP = 0, the raid is marked failed and dismissed

    💀 Boss Phases

    As the raid boss loses HP it gets stronger. The encounter ATK multiplier increases each phase — make sure your gear can keep up.

    Phase 1
    HP > 66%
    1.0× ATK

    Normal difficulty — use this phase to get your party organized.

    Phase 2
    HP 33–66%
    1.4× ATK

    Boss rages — encounter hits 40% harder. Gear becomes more important.

    Phase 3
    HP < 33%
    1.8× ATK

    Final push — encounter hits 80% harder. Only strong builds survive.

    Party Rules

    • Only one active raid per player at a time
    • Each party member can attack once per day
    • Winning the daily encounter deals contribution_per_win damage to the shared boss HP
    • Losing the daily encounter deals 0 damage — day is wasted
    • Invited players join the existing raid — they do NOT burn tribute
    • A live HP bar is shown on the Home screen while a raid is active

    💡 Tips & Strategy

    Attack Daily

    Missing a day means 0 damage for the party that day. Coordinate with party members to keep up consistent pressure on the boss HP.

    Gear for Phase 3

    The encounter hits 1.8× harder in Phase 3. Make sure your HP and HP Regen can survive the Eternal Titan at full rage (ATK 32 × 1.8 = 57.6).

    Save Mythic Tribute

    Eternal Raid requires 2× Mythic items. Mythic items are rare — prioritize crafting or opening chests to stockpile before attempting Eternal.

    Track HP on Home

    When a raid is active a live HP bar appears on the Home screen showing tier, phase, and boss HP%. Use it to gauge how many attacks are left.
