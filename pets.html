    🐾 Pets

    Pets are companions that buff your skill XP, help in arena combat, go on adventures to find materials, and grow alongside you over time. Choose your first pet for free when you start.

    There are two pets to choose from. Both buff all skills equally. Their appearance changes at every level — 10 unique portraits from LVL 1 to LVL 10.

    PetLVL 1 EmojiLVL 10 (Evolved)BuffsBase XP Buff (LVL 1, full hunger)

    🐕 Dog 🐕🐩 All Skills · Arena HP Regen · ATK · Gold% +5%

    Legendary Bond at LVL 10: Cat gives +2% XP to all skills · Dog gives +10% gold from arena kills.

    XP Buff Formula

    Your pet multiplies skill XP earned during work sessions.

    effectiveBuff = baseBuffPct × hungerFactor × levelFactor
    hungerFactor = 1.0 (if hunger ≥ 50%) or hunger / 50 (if hunger < 50%)
    levelFactor = 1 + (level − 1) × 0.1
    finalMultiplier = 1 + effectiveBuff + bondBonus + legendaryBuff
    (×1.5 during Motivation Burst ability)

    Cat or Dog at LVL 10, full hunger (≥50%): 5% × 1.0 × 1.9 = +9.5% XP to all skills. At 25% hunger: 5% × 0.5 × 1.9 = +4.75%.

    After the day-30 bond milestone, an additional +1% is permanently added. Cat's Legendary Bond adds another +2% at LVL 10.

    Arena Combat Buffs

    Global arena stats added based on pet level. All values scale with hunger (0 when starving or on adventure).

    +HP Regen unlocks at LVL 3
    +ATK unlocks at LVL 5
    +Gold% unlocks at LVL 7

    Your pet's mood reflects its hunger level and recent interactions. Moods are cosmetic — they don't change buff values (hunger does).

    😄Ecstatic— hunger ≥ 75%
    😊Happy— hunger 50–74%
    🥳Playful!— within 30 min of feeding or petting
    😴Sleeping— no interaction for 8+ hours (still gives buff if fed)
    😐Hungry— hunger 25–49%
    😢Starving— hunger 1–24% · buff severely reduced
    💀Collapsed— hunger 0% · no buff, can't adventure

    Pet the active pet up to 10 times per day (+1 XP, +2% hunger, triggers Playful mood for 30 min).
