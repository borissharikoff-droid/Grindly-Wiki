    🐾 Pets

    Pets are companions that buff your skill XP, help in arena combat, go on adventures to find materials, and grow alongside you over time. Roll your first pet for free — re-rolls cost 5,000 gold.

    Cat and Dog are starter-only (free first roll). All other species come from the gacha re-roll pool. Rarity affects roll odds and determines base buff strength.

    XP Buff Formula

    Your pet multiplies skill XP earned during work sessions.

    effectiveBuff = baseBuffPct × hungerFactor × levelFactor
    hungerFactor = 1.0 (if hunger ≥ 50%) or hunger / 50 (if hunger < 50%)
    levelFactor = 1 + (level − 1) × 0.1
    finalMultiplier = 1 + effectiveBuff + bondBonus + legendaryBuff
    (×1.5 during Motivation Burst ability)

    At LVL 10, full hunger, an Epic pet (12% base) gives: 12% × 1.0 × 1.9 = +22.8% XP to its skill.

    A Mythic Phoenix (20% base) at full buff: 20% × 1.9 = +38% XP to all skills.

    Arena Combat Buffs

    Global arena stats added based on pet level. All values scale with hunger (0 when starving or on adventure).

    Your pet's mood reflects its hunger level and recent interactions. Moods are cosmetic — they don't change buff values (hunger does).

    😄Ecstatic— hunger ≥ 75%
    😊Happy— hunger 50–74%
    🥳Playful!— within 30 min of feeding or petting
    😴Sleeping— no interaction for 8+ hours (still gives buff if fed)
    😐Hungry— hunger 25–49%
    😢Starving— hunger 1–24% · buff severely reduced
    💀Collapsed— hunger 0% · no buff, can't adventure

    Pet the active pet up to 10 times per day (+1 XP, +2% hunger, triggers Playful mood for 30 min).
