    Guilds

    Form a guild with friends, earn passive buffs, pool gold in the guild chest, and launch multi-day raids together.

    🏰 Creating a Guild

    Guilds are created from the Friends → Guild tab. Creating a guild costs 500 🪙 gold. You become the Owner automatically.

    🤝 Joining a Guild

    You can join a guild in two ways: browse public guilds in the Guild tab or accept an invite sent by an Officer+ member via your Friend's profile page.

    Browse & Join

    Open Friends → Guild → Browse. Search by name or browse top guilds sorted by chest gold. Click any guild to join instantly (if not already in one).

    Accept an Invite

    Officers and the Owner can invite friends directly from their Friend Profile page. Pending invites appear at the top of your Guild tab — accept or decline.

    ⚡ Guild Passive Buffs

    Simply being in a guild grants you two permanent passive bonuses — no action required. Buffs apply instantly on join and are removed on leave.

    ⚡
    +5% Skill XP
    All skill XP earned from focus sessions is multiplied by 1.05. Applies to all 12 skills — Developer, Designer, Gamer, etc.
    🪙
    +5% Arena Gold
    Gold earned from mob kills in the Arena is multiplied by 1.05. Stacks additively with Hot Zone and food bonuses.

    💰 Guild Tax

    The Owner can set a tax rate from 0% to 15%. Tax is automatically deducted from every arena mob kill gold reward and deposited into the Guild Chest.

    How Tax Works

    1. Owner sets tax rate (0–15%) via the slider in the Guild tab
    2. Player kills an arena mob — e.g. earns 1000g
    3. At 10% tax: 100g goes to Guild Chest, player keeps 900g
    4. Tax contribution counted toward member contribution score

    Tax range: 0% (off) → 15% (maximum). Default is 0%.

    🎁 Guild Chest

    The guild chest pools gold contributed by all members through tax and manual deposits. Any member can deposit gold manually at any time.

    👥 Member Management

    The Owner can manage all members. Officers can kick regular members. Member list shows contribution gold, online status, and current activity.

    Kick a Member

    Owner/Officer: open member list, click the member row, tap Kick. An inline confirmation (✓ ✗) appears before the action is final.

    Promote / Demote

    Owner only. Promote a member to Officer or demote an Officer back to Member via the member row actions.

    View All Members

    By default the list shows 12 members. Tap + X more to expand the full roster. Tap ▲ show less to collapse.

    Activity Log

    Every join, leave, and gold deposit is recorded in the guild's activity log (last 20 events), visible to all members.

    🚪 Leaving a Guild

    Any member can leave at any time. You immediately lose the +5% XP and +5% gold buffs. Contributed gold stays in the chest.

    ⚠️ Owners cannot leave their guild without first transferring ownership or disbanding. To disband, remove all members first.
