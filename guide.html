    What is Grindly?

    Grindly is a Windows desktop app that lives in your system tray and silently watches which apps you use. Every minute you spend coding, designing, gaming, or communicating earns XP in a matching skill — turning your real daily work into an RPG character sheet.

    There is no goal to distract you from real work. Grindly rewards you for doing it. The longer you focus, the more XP you earn, the better loot you unlock, and the harder bosses you can fight.

    ☀️
    Zero distraction designGrindly runs in the background and tracks everything automatically. You never need to open it. The tray icon glows when a session is active.

    Install & Launch

    Getting Grindly running takes under two minutes. It's a standard Windows installer — no account required to start tracking.

    • 1 Download the latest .exe installer from grindly.app or the Download for Windows button on this page.
    • 2 Run the installer. Windows may show a SmartScreen prompt — click "More info" → "Run anyway". Grindly is not signed yet (coming soon).
    • 3 Grindly launches and appears in your system tray (bottom-right corner of your taskbar). Look for the orange/gold G icon.
    • 4 Click the tray icon to open the main window. You'll land on the Home screen showing your active session.
    • 5 Optional: Create a free account (Settings → Account) to unlock leaderboards, friends, cloud sync, and the marketplace.
    💡
    Tip!Grindly starts with Windows automatically. You don't need to remember to launch it — just work normally and XP accumulates in the background.

    Activity Tracking

    Grindly uses a lightweight PowerShell subprocess to read the active foreground window every ~1.5 seconds. It never reads your screen contents, keystrokes, or any data inside your apps — only the process name and window title.

    Stats → Analytics — daily skill breakdown

    The Stats page shows a complete history of every session broken down by skill and day. You can see exactly how much time went to each category.

    Sessions start automatically when you begin working and end after 5 minutes of inactivity. Each completed session awards XP proportional to the time spent.

    💡
    Tip!A session only counts if you're actively using the keyboard or mouse. Pure video watching awards Listener XP, not general XP.

    Skills & XP

    Skills tab — each bar = one skill

    Grindly has 13 skills across two categories:

    Passive Skills — earned automatically based on which apps you use:

    💻 Developer 🎨 Designer 🎮 Gamer 💬 Communicator 🔎 Researcher 🌟 Creator 🎓 Learner 🎧 Listener

    Active Skills — earned inside Grindly:

    ⚔️ Warrior 🌿 Farmer 🔨 Crafter 🍳 Cook 🦋 Trader

    Each skill goes from Level 1 to 99 using a non-linear XP curve — early levels are fast, late levels require serious grind. At Level 99 you can prestige for bonus XP multipliers.

    ⚡
    XP FormulaHigher levels need exponentially more XP. Level 50 requires ~700K XP total; Level 99 requires 3.6M. See the full formula on the Skills page.

    Loot & Chests

    When a session ends, Grindly awards a loot chest based on how long and how focused your session was. Open it to receive random gear, materials, potions, or seeds.

    Chest opening animation

    There are 4 chest tiers:

    • ■ Common — short sessions (~15 min). Basic gear and materials.
    • ■ Rare — medium sessions (~45 min). Better gear, more materials.
    • ■ Epic — long sessions (~2h). Epic items, crafting materials, seeds.
    • ■ Legendary — marathon sessions (~4h+). Legendary/mythic gear, rare materials.
    💡
    Tip!You have a pity system — if you haven't gotten a legendary item in a while, your next chest has increased odds. Read the Loot page for exact drop rates.

    Inventory & Gear

    Inventory — character stats + items

    Open the Inventory tab to manage your collected items. Your character has 4 gear slots:

    • △ Head — helms, crowns, cosmetic headgear
    • ● Body — plates, robes, armor
    • ◇ Legs — greaves, trousers
    • ♢ Ring — rings, amulets, pendants

    Each equipped item contributes ATK, HP, HP Regen, and DEF to your combat stats. Better gear = stronger Arena performance.

    Item Power (IP) is a single number summarizing an item's strength. It's calculated from its rarity and perks. Use IP to quickly compare upgrades.

    ⚿
    Item Rarities — Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic. Higher rarity = more powerful perks and higher IP.

    Arena & Bosses

    Arena — boss combat in Slime Cavern

    The Arena tab is where your gear gets tested. Fight through 8 progressive zones, each with normal mobs and a zone boss.

    Combat is turn-based auto-battle. Your ATK, HP, and HP Regen stats (from equipped gear) determine how the fight plays out. You win by killing the boss before your HP runs out.

    Defeating bosses awards:

    • XP Warrior XP for your combat skill
    • 🌟 Gold used in the marketplace
    • 🏦 Materials used in crafting recipes
    ⚠️
    Gear requirement! Each zone has a minimum ATK/HP requirement. You can't enter a zone until your equipped gear meets the threshold. Upgrade your gear first.

    Zones unlock progressively: Slime Cavern → Goblin Camp → Orc Fortress → Dragon Lair → Shadow Crypt → Celestial Spire → Storm Peak → Void Realm. Each zone is harder but drops better materials.

    Crafting

    Craft tab — recipes unlock by Crafter level

    The Craft tab lets you combine materials from farming, arena, and chests into powerful gear, potions, and special items.

    Recipes require:

    • LVL A minimum Crafter level to unlock
    • MAT Specific materials in your inventory
    • T Real time to craft (5s to 2 hours)
    • 🌟 Optional gold cost for advanced recipes

    Crafting awards Crafter XP. Higher Crafter level unlocks more powerful recipes including the endgame Lich Set and Titan Set.

    💡
    Tip! Craft Compost first (Wheat + Herbs) — it's free, fast, and gives Crafter XP early. Then work toward Iron Bar and Fang Shard for your first gear upgrades.

    Marketplace

    Marketplace — player-to-player trading

    The Marketplace is a player-to-player trading system. List items you don't need, buy items you do. Prices are set by players and governed by supply and demand.

    • 1 Tap an item in your inventory and select "List on Market" to create a listing.
    • 2 Set your price in gold and quantity.
    • 3 Other players browsing the Marketplace can buy your listing instantly.
    • 4 You can hold up to 5 active listings at once. Listings expire after 7 days.

    Hover over the price sparkline on any item to see recent sale history — useful for pricing your items competitively.

    🌟
    Gold tip!Earn gold by defeating Arena bosses and selling excess items. Gold is the only currency — there are no premium purchases in Grindly.

    Pro Tips

    These are the things veteran Grindly players wish they'd known at the start:

    • 💡
      Equip your best gear before Arena runs. Your stats are read at the start of combat — always check your inventory first.
    • 💡
      Farm during long work sessions. Plant seeds before a big coding session — they'll be ready to harvest by the time you're done.
    • 💡
      Craft potions early. ATK and HP potions give a temporary combat boost that can make the difference in tough boss fights.
    • 💡
      Check the Marketplace before crafting. Sometimes it's faster and cheaper to buy materials than farm them yourself.
    • 💡
      Prestige at Level 99. Prestiged skills earn a permanent XP multiplier. Prestige your most-used skill first for maximum benefit.
    • 💡
      Use Focus Mode. Enable Focus Mode (tray right-click menu) to pause tracking during breaks — XP only counts when you're actually working.
    • 💡
      Connect your account. Account creation unlocks the friends list, global leaderboard, and cross-device cloud sync.
